    Manual M-Pesa payment guide

    This simple manual payment guide helps you send the processing fee to receive your bursary. Before you send money, check the recipient's name on your phone. Never enter or share your M-Pesa PIN on this website.

    1. Open M-Pesa on your phone.
    2. Choose Send money.
    3. Enter the number 0732532860.
    4. Enter the amount .
    5. Enter your M-Pesa PIN and send.
    Read before you paste your M-Pesa message
    • Copy and paste the whole M-Pesa message exactly as it appears on your phone.
    • Do not crop the message.
    • Do not edit any word, number or punctuation in the message.
    • Our system checks every character. If it detects cropping, editing or a fake message, you will be banned from getting this bursary.
